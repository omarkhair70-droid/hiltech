# Wireflow — New Hire & Offboarding

Status: EXPERIENCE WIREFLOW v0.1

## Part A — New Hire

```text
Hire Approved
   ↓
Identity Invite
   ↓
New Hire Mobile
   ↓
Profile
Documents
Bank/payroll info
Policies
Training
   ↓
Manager/Team
   ↓
Access
   ↓
Tools/PPE/Device if needed
   ↓
First Day
   ↓
ACTIVE
```

---

# Progressive Home

Before active:
Home = onboarding checklist.

After active:
Home transforms into actual role experience.

Example technician:
Today/Jobs.

No separate onboarding app.

---

# Blockers

If missing:
- document.
- training.
- manager.
- access.
- asset/PPE.
- payroll data.

Show:
```text
3 of 7 complete
2 waiting on HILTECH
2 need you
```

User shouldn't be blamed for internal blockers.

---

# First Day

Shows:
- location/time.
- manager.
- team.
- required training.
- issued assets.
- first project/work if ready.

# Part C — Offboarding

```text
Start Offboarding
    ↓
Coordinated Case
    ↓
Project transfer

Warehouse clearance

Expense/advance settle
Final payroll
HR documents
    ↓
All mandatory conditions
    ↓
FORMER
```

---

# Critical Access Revocation

Security action can happen earlier than administrative completion.

```text
Offboarding started
  ↓
Policy says immediate revoke
  ↓
Sessions/devices/access revoked
  ↓
Other clearance continues
```

Do not wait for physical paperwork to protect system access.

---

# Unreturned Asset

```text
Offboarding
 ↓
Asset clearance:
Fluke 03 still with employee
 ↓
Cannot silently complete
 ↓
Return / incident / approved exception
```

---

# Final Pay

Payroll/finance sees offboarding context but HR does not manually edit closed payroll history.

Final settlement follows payroll rules.

---

# Success Criteria

Hire:
new person knows exactly what remains.

Offboarding:
no account, project, tool, or finance obligation disappears because someone forgot a checklist.
