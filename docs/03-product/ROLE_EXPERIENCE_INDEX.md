# HILTECH Complete Role Experience Index

Status: ACTIVE / GROWING

Purpose:
Translate each Human Map persona into a complete product experience connected to HILTECH objects, permissions, events, automation, notifications, mobile/desktop behavior, offline rules, errors, and cross-role handoffs.

## Rule
A Persona Map explains the person's reality.
A Role Experience defines how HILTECH serves that reality.
They are intentionally separate artifacts.
