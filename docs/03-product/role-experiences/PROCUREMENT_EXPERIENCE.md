# Complete Role Experience — Procurement

Status: PRODUCT MODEL v0.1 / NOT BUILD-READY

## Product thesis
Procurement should operate from validated company demand, not chat messages, while preserving quote history and commercial traceability.

## Desktop home
- New requirements
- Need info
- Sourcing
- Quotes received
- Comparisons
- Approval
- PO pending
- Deliveries
- Late orders
- Invoice mismatches

## Mobile
- urgent requirement
- quote/PO context
- supplier contact
- approval status
- delivery update

## Key commands
- validate requirement
- request supplier quote
- record quote/version
- compare suppliers
- submit approval
- create/issue PO
- amend PO
- track confirmation/delivery
- resolve mismatch/claim

## Automation
- stock availability check
- quote-expiry reminder
- late-delivery warning
- PO/receipt/invoice matching
- supplier factual history metrics

## Edge states
- emergency purchase
- partial delivery
- supplier changes price after approval
- wrong item
- cancelled project after PO
- duplicate requirement
- invoice before receipt

## Cross-role
Project/Warehouse -> need.
Procurement -> supplier.
Warehouse/site -> receipt.
Finance -> payable.
Owner/PM -> policy approval.

## Success
Every purchase can be traced from business need to receipt/payment context.
