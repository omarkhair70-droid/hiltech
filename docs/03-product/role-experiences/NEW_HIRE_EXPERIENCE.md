# Complete Role Experience — New Hire

Status: PRODUCT MODEL v0.1 / NOT BUILD-READY

## Product thesis
A new employee should enter one guided HILTECH experience that progressively becomes their normal role experience.

## Before first day
- Welcome
- Profile
- Required documents
- bank/payroll details where applicable
- policy/safety acknowledgement
- manager/team
- first-day location/time

## First day
- account/access activation
- team/context
- role guide
- required training
- assigned equipment/PPE
- first project/site if applicable

## Progressive disclosure
The new hire should not see the whole OS. Features appear as their employment state, role, and permissions become valid.

## Mobile-first
This experience should work well from a personal Android device unless company policy requires managed hardware.

## Key states
INVITED / ACTIVATED / PREBOARDING / FIRST_DAY / IN_PROGRESS / COMPLETE / BLOCKED

## Blockers
- missing document
- missing manager/role
- bank/payroll info
- required safety/training
- asset/PPE issue
- access not provisioned

## Notifications
- invitation
- missing requirement
- first-day reminder
- training/asset appointment
- onboarding complete

## Success
The employee knows exactly what remains before they can work normally.
