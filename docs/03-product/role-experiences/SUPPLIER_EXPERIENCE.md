# Complete Role Experience — Supplier

Status: PRODUCT MODEL v0.1 / NOT BUILD-READY

## Product thesis
Supplier experience should make commercial exchange traceable while exposing only supplier-owned context.

## Home
- RFQs
- Purchase Orders
- Amendments
- Upcoming deliveries
- Delivery issues
- Invoice status

## Key commands
- respond to RFQ
- confirm PO
- propose/confirm delivery date
- report shortage/backorder
- upload delivery/invoice documents
- respond to rejection/return

## Permissions
Supplier sees own:
- RFQs
- quotes
- POs
- deliveries
- invoices
- returns/claims

Supplier never sees competitor quote, HILTECH margin, unrelated project financials, or other suppliers.

## Notifications
- RFQ
- PO/amendment
- delivery reminder
- discrepancy/rejection
- invoice status/action

## Edge states
- partial PO acceptance
- delayed/backordered item
- changed delivery destination
- amendment after supplier confirmation
- invoice mismatch
- replacement/credit note

## Success
Order history and delivery truth no longer depend on phone calls and detached documents.
