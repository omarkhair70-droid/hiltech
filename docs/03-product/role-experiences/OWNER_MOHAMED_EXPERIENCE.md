# Complete Role Experience — Mohamed / Owner

Status: PRODUCT MODEL v0.1 / NOT BUILD-READY

## Product thesis
Mohamed should not manage HILTECH by chasing people. HILTECH should continuously compress company reality into decisions, exceptions, risk, and strategic context.

His experience is designed around:
- What needs me?
- What is at risk?
- What changed?
- Where is money blocked?
- Which client/project matters now?
- What can the system/team handle without me?

## Primary experience mode
Management by exception.

Routine operational events should change company state without becoming direct owner work.

# 1. Mobile Home — Company

## Top section
### Needs You
Prioritized owner-only actions:
- approvals requiring owner authority
- high-value payment/payroll approval
- exceptional commercial decision
- major project/client escalation
- critical security/warehouse exception

Each card shows:
- what
- why owner is required
- risk/impact
- deadline
- responsible person
- direct action

## Company Pulse
- active projects
- projects at risk
- active sites today
- field people working
- critical issues
- expected cash in/out where authorized
- receivables/payables requiring attention
- high-value assets at risk/overdue if meaningful

## Today at HILTECH
Compressed activity summary:
- major milestones
- new awards
- critical procurement
- payments
- client escalations
- operational anomalies

No raw event flood.

# 12. Cross-device continuity

Example:
Ahmed prepares payroll on Desktop.
Mohamed receives owner approval on Mobile.
Mohamed approves.
Ahmed's Desktop changes immediately to APPROVED.
No file/message handoff.

Another:
Mohamed opens Project A on Desktop.
Later Mobile shows recent context/quick access, not remote desktop.

---

# 13. Offline

Read-only cached executive context may be available offline.

Critical actions such as payment/payroll/high-risk approval require authoritative online confirmation.

Never show stale data as live without visible freshness status.

---

# 14. Empty / Error / Edge States

Must design:
- no owner actions pending
- stale/offline company pulse
- data source/integration unavailable
- approval object changed since opened
- payment/financial source unknown
- project metric incomplete
- user permission recently changed
- camera/NVR unavailable
- partial system outage

---

# 15. Notifications

Owner should receive only:
- owner action required
- critical company exception
- major client/project escalation
- high-risk financial/security event
- optional digest

All other events remain discoverable in activity/search but do not interrupt.

# 18. Success criteria

The experience succeeds when:
- Mohamed can understand company health in minutes.
- Routine operations do not require him.
- Important decisions arrive with enough context.
- He can act from mobile safely.
- Desktop gives deeper control without becoming a second product.
- Nothing critical depends on WhatsApp memory or one person's verbal summary.
