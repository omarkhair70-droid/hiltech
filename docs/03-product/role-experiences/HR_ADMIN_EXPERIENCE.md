# Complete Role Experience — HR / Admin

Status: PRODUCT MODEL v0.1 / NOT BUILD-READY

## Product thesis
HR/Admin should orchestrate employee lifecycle and exceptions while HILTECH propagates identity, role, permission, access, and asset implications automatically.

## Desktop home
- New hires
- Missing documents
- Leave
- Attendance exceptions
- Expiring certifications/docs
- Role/team changes
- Offboarding
- Access/asset clearance

## Mobile
- urgent employee action
- leave approval where assigned
- onboarding status
- expiring item
- offboarding exception

## Key commands
- invite new hire
- verify documents
- activate employee
- change role/team/manager
- process leave
- manage certification/training
- start offboarding
- confirm HR clearance

## Automation
- onboarding checklist by role
- default permission proposal
- expiry reminders
- offboarding access revocation
- asset-return checklist
- payroll eligibility propagation

## Sensitive boundaries
HR identity/personal data must not leak into PM/finance views beyond necessary context.

## Edge states
- incomplete documents
- employee starts before full onboarding
- role changed mid-project
- employee has unreturned asset at offboarding
- payroll period overlaps termination
- access revocation failure
- duplicate identity

## Success
Joining, changing role, and leaving the company do not depend on people remembering scattered checklists.
