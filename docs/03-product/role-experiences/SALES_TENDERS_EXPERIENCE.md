# Complete Role Experience — Sales / Tenders

Status: PRODUCT MODEL v0.1 / NOT BUILD-READY

## Product thesis
Sales/Tenders should turn opportunity into structured delivery truth without a dead handoff.

## Desktop home
- Leads/opportunities
- Tender calendar
- Site visits
- Quotes in progress
- Internal approvals
- Submitted bids
- Negotiations
- Won/lost
- Existing-client expansion

## Mobile
- meeting/site visit
- contact/client history
- quick notes/photos
- follow-up
- approval status
- tender deadline alerts

## Key commands
- create/qualify opportunity
- schedule discovery/site visit
- capture scope/assumptions
- prepare quote/tender version
- request commercial approval
- submit/revise
- mark award/loss with evidence/reason
- initiate delivery handoff

## Automation
- deadline reminders
- missing requirement detection
- quote version control
- won opportunity seeds project
- expansion opportunity from delivered client/site

## Edge states
- client changes scope after quote
- tender deadline moves
- conflicting quote versions
- partner/subcontract route
- opportunity won before final contract
- handoff missing critical scope information

## Success
Winning a job should create delivery-ready structured context without re-entering the same client/scope/site data.
