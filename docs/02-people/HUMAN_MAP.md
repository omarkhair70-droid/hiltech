# HILTECH OS — Human Map

Status: `RESEARCHING`

This map starts from people and work, not screens.

For every persona we must eventually document:

1. what starts their day,
2. information they need,
3. decisions they make,
4. work they perform,
5. what they currently chase manually,
6. what the system can eliminate,
7. what they send to others,
8. what they receive from others,
9. permissions and confidentiality,
10. mobile/desktop usage,
11. offline needs,
12. important alerts,
13. success criteria.

## 1. Mohamed — Owner / Company Control

### Core objective

Understand company health and intervene only where authority, risk, money, client escalation, or exception requires him.

### Product idea

HILTECH should bring Mohamed decisions, not force Mohamed to hunt through the system.

Potential areas:

- company pulse,
- projects at risk,
- key receivables/payables,
- approvals,
- payroll approval,
- high-value purchase approval,
- client escalations,
- warehouse/security exceptions,
- high-level people/operations view,
- remote access to authorized office/security context,
- company activity timeline.

### Principle

`Management by exception`.

## 2. Ahmed — Finance / Admin Control

### Core objective

Control money and administrative flows without repetitive manual entry.

Potential areas:

- cash/bank position,
- receivables/payables,
- payroll preparation,
- employee advances/deductions/reimbursements,
- supplier invoices,
- client invoices,
- payment preparation,
- reconciliation,
- project costing,
- approval routing,
- payroll/payslip history,
- tax/admin documents depending on real responsibility.

### Cross-domain requirement

Finance should consume legitimate events from work, warehouse, procurement and people rather than re-entering the same data.

## Cross-persona law

A persona is not a separate application.

The same object (project, asset, invoice, site, work order) can be seen differently according to identity, relationship, permission and context while remaining the same underlying record.
