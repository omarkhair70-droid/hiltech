# New Hire — Experience Map

Status: RESEARCHING / DESIGN HYPOTHESIS

## Product goal
A person joining HILTECH should understand who they are in the company, what they need to complete, where to go, who they report to, what they are responsible for, and what tools/access they have without depending on scattered verbal instructions.

## Invitation
The person receives an official HILTECH invite and activates the same HILTECH application used after employment.

## Pre-first-day experience
- Welcome.
- Confirm profile.
- Submit required documents.
- Payroll/bank data where appropriate.
- Read essential company policies.
- Safety/field requirements.
- See manager/team.
- See first-day time/location/contact.
- Complete required acknowledgements.

## First-day experience
- arrival/context
- identity/access activation if applicable
- team introduction
- role summary
- first required training
- assigned equipment
- first project/site if known
- support/contact path

## Progressive disclosure
Do not expose the entire HILTECH OS to a new person. Their experience grows with role and lifecycle state.

## Role-aware onboarding examples
### Technician
Safety + field workflow + QR/assets + assigned tools + Today.

### Engineer
Projects + drawings + technical evidence + tests.

### Finance employee
Finance policy + approvals + sensitive-data rules.

## Completion
Onboarding is complete only when required company, access, role, equipment, and compliance conditions for that employee category are satisfied.

## Events
- invite.sent
- account.activated
- onboarding.step.completed
- document.submitted
- policy.acknowledged
- equipment.assigned
- manager.confirmed
- onboarding.completed
