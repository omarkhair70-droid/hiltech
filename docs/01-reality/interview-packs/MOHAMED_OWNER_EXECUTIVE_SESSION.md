# Interview Pack — Mohamed / Owner & Executive

Status: **READY FOR REALITY SESSION**
Purpose: validate HILTECH's real management/authority model before product and policy freeze.

This is a guided conversation, not a form.
Do not collect secrets that are not needed for product design.

---

# Session 1 — What Mohamed Actually Does

Ask for one normal recent day/week:
- What did people call/message you about?
- What decisions only you made?
- What did you approve?
- What did you check because you did not trust the current process?
- What did you chase manually?
- What did you discover too late?
- What do you want to stop being interrupted for?

Output:
- Needs You queue candidates,
- Company Pulse signals,
- delegation opportunities,
- automation opportunities.

---

# Session 2 — Authority Map

For each category ask who can:
- prepare,
- recommend,
- approve,
- reject,
- change after approval,
- act when Mohamed is unavailable.

Categories:
- payroll,
- employee advance / سلفة,
- financial imprest / عهدة,
- supplier payment,
- procurement/PO,
- project variation,
- hiring/offboarding,
- stock adjustment,
- asset retirement/loss,
- client commitments,
- security/access changes.

Need:
- thresholds if any,
- exact delegations,
- re-auth/high-risk actions,
- exceptions.

Do not invent numeric approval thresholds if Mohamed does not use them today.

Validate current internal report:

- What absolutely has to work before any Client Portal?

Ahmed + PM + field + warehouse + owner control are core; full Client Portal is last.

# Session 4 — Project Health

Ask:
- How do you know a project is healthy today?
- What report/message do you trust?
- What makes you visit/call?
- What are the earliest warning signs?
- Which blockers deserve your attention vs PM attention?
- How do you know progress is real, not optimistic reporting?

Map signals:
- delayed work,
- missing material,
- missing tool,
- client approval waiting,
- drawing/revision issue,
- cash/collection risk,
- subcontractor issue,
- rework/snags.

Output:
Company Pulse + Project Command Center exception model.

# Session 5 — Money Pulse

Ask what Mohamed actually wants to know, not every accounting field:
- money expected in,
- overdue client money,
- major supplier/payment obligations,
- payroll ready/not ready,
- high-value approvals,
- project cost/risk,
- advances/imprest exceptions,
- failed/unknown payment.

Ask:
What financial fact would make you call Ahmed immediately?

Output:
Owner finance pulse and escalation rules.

# Session 6 — Warehouse / Assets

Ask:
- What warehouse/tool problem worries you most?
- Who has keys/access today?
- Which assets are too expensive to lose?
- What do you personally get involved in?
- What counts as acceptable checkout/return proof?
- What should happen if tool is missing/damaged/late?
- Who should be able to override asset custody?

Output:
asset/warehouse exception policies + physical access validation.

---

# Session 7 — Security / Facilities

Ask:
- Which cameras/doors/locations matter?
- What can Mohamed see today?
- Which events deserve alert?
- Who may access camera/security data?

Need actual vendor/system inventory later.
