# HILTECH Reality Validation Interview Pack

Status: READY TO USE

Purpose:
Close the remaining company-specific unknowns quickly with Mohamed, Ahmed, and selected staff.

This is not a generic questionnaire.
Only answers that materially affect product/schema/permissions/integrations are included.

---

# A. Mohamed — Owner

## 1. Company control
- What do you personally check every day?
- What do people interrupt you for most?
- What approvals must reach you?
- What approvals can someone else take?
- Are there amount thresholds for PO/payment/advance/imprest/discount/write-off?
- Can you delegate temporarily? To whom?
- What do you want to see on your phone vs desktop?

## 2. Organization
- Who are the real department/function owners today?
- Who reports to whom in practice?
- Who can create projects?
- Who can assign technicians?
- Who can approve procurement?
- Who can approve payroll?
- Who can approve payments?
- Who can retire/write off company assets?

## 3. Projects
- Pick one normal recent project and explain:
  award -> kickoff -> team -> site -> work -> tests -> handover -> payment.
- What makes you consider a project at risk?
- What reports do you receive today?
- What do you still discover late?

## 4. Warehouse
- Who has warehouse authority today?
- Who has the physical key/access?
- Can technicians take tools without warehouse signoff?
- What high-value tools matter most?
- Are tools frequently lost/delayed/unreturned?
- Is calibration tracked today?

## 5. Financial control
- What payments require you?
- Payroll: do you approve total only or details?
- Advances / سلف: who approves?
- Financial imprest / عهدة مالية: who receives it, for what, and how is it settled?
- What happens if money/receipts are missing?

## 6. Security
- What cameras/NVR are installed?
- What access-control/biometric systems exist?
- Do you want HILTECH to expose cameras in the app?
- Who may view cameras/access logs?
- Does warehouse need controlled electronic access?

## 7. Priority
Confirm:
- internal company OS first.
- client external portal last.
- which internal area hurts most today: finance / projects / warehouse / field / HR / sales?
