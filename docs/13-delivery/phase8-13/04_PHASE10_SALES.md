# P10 Sales/Tenders owner / branch contract
10A Opportunity, customer org, RFP/tender and deadlines, authorized owner, site visit and restricted source document versions.
10B Scope/BOQ versions, source-priced item and labor cost, restricted internal margin, commercial quote versions/approval and client-visible quotation release without cost leak.
10C signed client Contract and Client PO, source-bound awarded version, Won -> existing Project/Site/Work conversion with no duplicate project; publish billing milestones/terms for Finance and coverage/warranty for Support.
Reuse Organizations, Projects, Approval, Evidence, Audit, Inbox, communication abstraction. Independent of Payroll/Finance payout. Do not reuse procurement supplier quote as a customer quotation or infer prices.
Hard gate example real BOQ/quotes/contracts, sales authority, signoff rules, price/cost segregation, corrections and loss/withdrawn tender history. Native sales desktop, bounded mobile visit capture where justified.
